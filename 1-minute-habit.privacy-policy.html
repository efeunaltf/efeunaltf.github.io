Privacy Policy

1 Minute Habit App

1. Information We Collect

1 Minute Habit is designed with privacy in mind. We collect minimal information to provide you with the best habit-tracking experience:

  • Habit Data: Your habit names, descriptions, completion records, and streak information are stored locally on your device.
  • Subscription Information: If you purchase premium features, RevenueCat processes your subscription data according to their privacy policy.
  • Device Information: Basic device information for app functionality and crash reporting.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to:

  • Track your habit progress and maintain streaks
  • Provide premium features if subscribed
  • Send local notifications for habit reminders
  • Improve app performance and fix bugs

3. Data Storage

All your habit data is stored locally on your device using secure storage mechanisms. We do not store your personal habit information on our servers. Your data remains private and under your control.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • RevenueCat: For subscription management and in-app purchases. Please review RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple App Store: For app distribution and payment processing on iOS devices.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • With your explicit consent
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To protect our rights and safety

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information:

  • Local data encryption on your device
  • Secure communication protocols
  • Regular security updates

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data (available in the app)
  • Delete your data (reset app data in settings)
  • Export your data (contact us for assistance)
  • Opt-out of notifications (in app settings)

8. Children's Privacy

Our app is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new privacy policy in the app and updating the "Last Updated" date below.
