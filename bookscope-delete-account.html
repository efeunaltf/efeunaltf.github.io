Delete Account

Bookscope - Your AI Book Assistant

⚠️ Important: Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data will be permanently removed from our servers.

What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data will be permanently removed:

  • Profile Information: Your name, email, and account details
  • Reading List: All books you've added to your list
  • Favorite Topics: Your selected interests and preferences
  • AI Chat History: All conversations with Booky AI
  • Book Analysis: All AI-generated book analyses and recommendations
  • Subscription Data: Your premium subscription status (if applicable)

How to Delete Your Account

1
Open the Bookscope app on your device
2
Go to Settings (tap the gear icon in the bottom navigation)
3
Scroll down to the Account section
4
Tap "Delete Account"
5
Confirm your decision and enter your password when prompted
6
Your account will be deleted immediately and you'll be logged out

Before You Delete

💡 Consider these alternatives:

  • If you're having issues with the app, try logging out and back in
  • If you're concerned about privacy, review our Privacy Policy
  • If you want to take a break, simply log out without deleting
  • If you have a premium subscription, cancel it before deleting your account
